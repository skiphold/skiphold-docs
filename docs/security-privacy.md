# Security and Privacy for the SkipHold White-Label AI Communications Platform

This page gives a high-level overview of how SkipHold protects partner and customer data. For portal roles, see the [Admin Portal Guide](admin-portal-guide.md).

## Multi-tenant data separation
- Every provider and company has its own agents, phone connections, conversations, call logs and settings.
- Users only see the providers and companies they've been given access to.

## Role-based access control
- Role-based access for Provider Admins, Company Admins and Employees.
- Individual permissions can be granted or removed per user.
- Sensitive account changes, such as changing your email or password, need email verification.
- Admins can sign users out of active sessions.

## Activity, audit and recording access logs
- Audit logs record administrative activity.
- Access to call recordings is logged: who listened to which recording.
- Failed sign-in attempts are logged and trigger email alerts.

## Data handling and retention
- Call recordings and conversation history are kept for limited retention periods.
- Separate test and live environments let you try changes without touching live customer traffic.

## FAQ

**Is my customers' data kept separate?**
Yes. Each provider and company has its own data, and users only see what they've been given access to.

**Who can listen to call recordings?**
Only users with access to that company. Every listen is recorded in the recording access log.

**Can I control what each user can do?**
Yes. Roles set the default permissions, and admins can grant or remove individual permissions per user.

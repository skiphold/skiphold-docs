# Getting Started with the SkipHold White-Label AI Communications Platform

This guide takes you from a new account to your first live AI agent. For a product overview, see [how SkipHold works](https://skiphold.com/how-it-works).

## What SkipHold is

SkipHold is a white-label AI communications platform that providers (MSPs, UCaaS providers, ITSPs and telecom partners) deliver end-to-end to their customers under their own brand. AI and human teams work every conversation together across voice, SMS and WhatsApp. The SkipHold team deploys, trains and supports the offering with you.

A few terms used throughout these docs:
- **Provider:** your organization, the partner reselling SkipHold.
- **Company:** one of your business customers.
- **AI agent:** a Receptionist, Manager or Assistant that answers and handles calls. See [AI voice agent types](https://skiphold.com/ai-voice-agent-types).
- **Phone connection:** how calls reach SkipHold: a carrier number, Gemelos PBX, or your own PBX / UCaaS platform.

## Step 1: Get access to the Admin Portal

1. [Contact the SkipHold team](https://skiphold.com/contact) or ask your SkipHold partner manager for an invite.
2. Sign in at https://admin.skiphold.com, or at your own branded portal domain once it's set up.
3. New accounts are reviewed and approved before they can use the portal. You'll get an email when your account is ready.

## Step 2: Set up your provider and company structure

- **Providers:** create a company for each customer. See [Provider & Partner Onboarding](partner-onboarding.md).
- **Company Admins:** add departments and employees. AI handoffs and transfers use this structure.

## Step 3: Create your first AI receptionist

1. Open **AI Agents** and create a **Receptionist**.
2. Work through the setup wizard:
   - **Basic Info:** name, voice and greeting.
   - **Personality:** tone and style.
   - **Context:** company overview, products and services, business hours.
   - **Instructions:** when and how to hand off or transfer.
3. Use **Prompt Tools™**, the visual prompt builder with templates and role-based context, to shape the agent's instructions.
4. Pick a role, such as General Receptionist, Appointment Setter, Lead Qualifier or After Hours, and a language (English, Spanish or Portuguese).
5. Use **Start Call** or **Start Chat** to test the agent before going live.

## Step 4: Connect a phone line

Choose one path:
- **Carrier number:** use a number from [Twilio](https://skiphold.com/integrations/twilio), Telnyx, Bandwidth or another carrier.
- **Gemelos PBX:** link your [Gemelos PBX](https://skiphold.com/native-pbx) tenant to the company.
- **Your PBX / UCaaS platform:** connect with SIP extension registration or a SIP trunk.

The [SIP Integration Overview](sip-integration.md) has the details. When the connection shows as active in the portal, place a test call.

## Step 5: Turn on routing, notifications and messaging

- Set up handoffs and [AI warm transfers](https://skiphold.com/warm-transfers) to departments and employees.
- Turn on email and SMS notifications: missed calls, call summaries, transfer alerts.
- Explore Conversations, Connections (CRM and help desk), Automations and Analytics in the [Admin Portal Guide](admin-portal-guide.md).
- Get ideas from [Common Use Cases](use-cases.md).
- Join the [SkipHold Community](https://community.skiphold.com) on Discord to connect with other MSPs, telecom providers and partners.

## FAQ

**Do my customers need new phones or hardware?**
No. SkipHold connects to your existing PBX, UCaaS platform or carrier over SIP.

**Which languages do AI agents speak?**
English, Spanish and Portuguese.

**Can I test an agent before it takes real calls?**
Yes. Every agent has **Start Call** and **Start Chat** test options in the portal.

**Where do I sign in?**
At https://admin.skiphold.com, or at your own branded domain once your provider portal is set up.

**Where can I get help or connect with other partners?**
Join the [SkipHold Community](https://community.skiphold.com) on Discord, email info@skiphold.com, or [contact our team](https://skiphold.com/contact). More answers are in the [SkipHold FAQ](https://skiphold.com/faq).

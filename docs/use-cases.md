# AI Receptionist Use Cases for MSPs, UCaaS Providers and ITSPs

Here's how providers use SkipHold, the white-label AI communications platform, with their customers, starting with the AI receptionist. Ready to set one up? Start with [Getting Started](getting-started.md).

## AI receptionist for MSPs: never miss a client call
- An always-on Receptionist answers support and sales lines, including after hours.
- A Support Intake agent captures the issue, contact details and urgency, then emails or texts a summary to your team.
- AI warm transfers hand urgent calls to an on-call technician along with an AI summary.
- More at [AI call routing for MSPs](https://skiphold.com/ai-call-routing-for-msps).

## AI receptionist for UCaaS providers: a value-added service
- Add AI agents to customer tenants on the NetSapiens, Thirdlane, 3CX, FreePBX or FusionPBX platforms you already run, over SIP. See the [SIP Integration Overview](sip-integration.md).
- Sell it under your brand, from your own portal domain, billed through Platform Billing or Direct Billing.
- Customers keep their extensions, ring groups and phones. Transfers go back through their PBX.
- More at [AI receptionist for UCaaS providers](https://skiphold.com/ai-receptionist-for-ucaas-providers).

## White-label AI call answering for ITSPs and VoIP resellers
- Point carrier numbers (Twilio, Telnyx, Bandwidth and other carriers) or SIP trunks straight at AI agents.
- Manage every customer company from one multi-tenant provider account.
- Offer AI SMS and WhatsApp alongside voice.
- More at [white-label AI receptionist](https://skiphold.com/white-label-ai-receptionist).

## Hosted PBX operators: native AI on Gemelos PBX
- Link Gemelos PBX tenants to SkipHold companies and turn on AI agents for each tenant.
- Agents appear in users' softphone directory so staff can reach them.
- More at [native PBX](https://skiphold.com/native-pbx).

## AI warm transfers and department routing
- Receptionist → department Manager → employee Assistant, then the right person.
- The person picking up hears an AI summary before the caller is connected.
- Each employee's personal Assistant takes calls for them when they're busy.
- More at [AI warm transfers](https://skiphold.com/warm-transfers).

## Lead capture and appointment setting
- Lead Qualifier and Appointment Setter agents qualify callers and book time via connected calendars, n8n / Zapier MCP automations or your own APIs.
- Leads, follow-ups and tickets are tracked in [Connections](https://skiphold.com/connections), the built-in CRM and help desk.
- Agents can send follow-ups by SMS or WhatsApp during the call.

## AI SMS and WhatsApp for providers
- Handle SMS and WhatsApp conversations in the same omni-channel [Conversations](https://skiphold.com/conversations) inbox as voice, with A2P 10DLC compliance support for US messaging.
- Staff take over from the AI when a person is needed.
- More at [AI SMS](https://skiphold.com/integrations/sms) and [AI WhatsApp](https://skiphold.com/integrations/whatsapp).

## Meeting Join™: AI agents in meetings
- AI agents join Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls by dialing in and entering meeting IDs/PINs via DTMF.
- More at [Meeting Join™](https://skiphold.com/ai-meeting-participant).

## Connecting legacy business systems
- Custom API tools and webhooks, built on request by the SkipHold team, connect agents with modern or legacy systems.

## FAQ

**What is an AI warm transfer?**
The AI agent calls the right person and gives them a short summary of the call before connecting the caller.

**Can the AI receptionist cover after-hours calls?**
Yes. The After Hours role is built for out-of-hours coverage, with summaries sent by email or SMS.

**Can AI agents join Zoom or Microsoft Teams meetings?**
Yes, with Meeting Join™. Agents dial in and enter meeting IDs/PINs via DTMF. Google Meet works the same way.

**Does SkipHold handle SMS and WhatsApp as well as voice?**
Yes. All three channels share one inbox, with human takeover.

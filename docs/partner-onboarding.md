# Partner Onboarding: White-Label AI Communications for MSPs and UCaaS Providers

SkipHold is a white-label AI communications platform you deliver end-to-end to your customers under your own brand, starting with the AI receptionist. SkipHold deploys, trains and supports the offering, so you don't have to build it. This guide covers setting up a provider account and onboarding your customers. Program details are on the [SkipHold partner program](https://skiphold.com/partners) page.

## Who partners with SkipHold

- MSPs and MSSPs (see [AI call routing for MSPs](https://skiphold.com/ai-call-routing-for-msps))
- UCaaS providers (see [AI receptionist for UCaaS providers](https://skiphold.com/ai-receptionist-for-ucaas-providers))
- ITSPs and VoIP providers
- Hosted PBX operators
- Telecom resellers and master agents

## Step 1: Set up your provider account

- SkipHold creates your provider account and invites your first Provider Admin.
- Invite your team under **Users**. Each person gets a role (Provider Admin, Company Admin or Employee) and you can override individual permissions per user.

## Step 2: Brand your white-label portal

Under **Branding**, set:
- your logo
- primary, secondary and accent colors for light and dark mode
- your custom portal domain
- the email sender name, reply-to address and footer used for notifications

A live preview shows your changes before you save. After that, your customers see your brand throughout the portal and in its emails. More at [white-label AI receptionist](https://skiphold.com/white-label-ai-receptionist).

## Step 3: Choose a billing mode

If the billing module is enabled for your account, choose how your customers are billed. Both modes are fully white-label: proposals, invoices, statements and the portal carry your branding. Billing is powered by Stripe.

- **Platform Billing:** charges are processed on SkipHold's Stripe account, so the card transaction shows "SkipHold, LLC" on the cardholder's statement. SkipHold handles collections and pays you your revenue share on the platform payout cycle.
- **Direct Billing:** you use your own Stripe account, so your own business name shows on the card statement. You pay SkipHold the platform fee.

You can also set your billing day of month, build a product catalog and bundles, and send proposals your customers can review and accept online. See [provider billing](https://skiphold.com/billing) and [pricing](https://skiphold.com/pricing).

## Step 4: Add your customer companies

For each customer:
1. Create the company and invite its Company Admin.
2. Connect its phone line: carrier number, Gemelos PBX, or the customer's PBX / UCaaS platform. See the [SIP Integration Overview](sip-integration.md).
3. Create its AI agents and departments, or let the Company Admin do it. See [Getting Started](getting-started.md).
4. Optionally turn on [Connections](https://skiphold.com/connections) (CRM and help desk) and Billing for the company.

## Step 5: Go live and grow

- Test every agent with a test call before launch.
- Use Call Logs, Conversations, live call monitoring and Analytics to review performance with your customer. See the [Admin Portal Guide](admin-portal-guide.md).
- Add Manager and Assistant agents as customers grow into department and personal routing.
- Ask the SkipHold team about custom API tools and webhooks for customers with special system needs.
- Join the [SkipHold Community](https://community.skiphold.com) on Discord to connect with other MSPs, telecom providers, AI builders and partners.

## FAQ

**Can I resell SkipHold under my own brand?**
Yes. SkipHold is a white-label AI communications platform: your logo, colors, custom domain, branded emails and white-label billing.

**What's the difference between Platform Billing and Direct Billing?**
In Platform Billing, charges run on SkipHold's Stripe account: the card transaction shows "SkipHold, LLC", and SkipHold pays you a revenue share. In Direct Billing, you use your own Stripe account: your business name shows on the card statement, and you pay SkipHold a platform fee. In both modes, proposals, invoices, statements and the portal carry your branding.

**Can I send proposals to prospects?**
Yes. Build proposals from your product catalog and bundles. Customers can review and accept them online.

**Which phone systems can my customers use?**
- Native: Gemelos PBX, SkipHold's own hosted PBX.
- SIP interoperability: NetSapiens, Thirdlane, 3CX, FreePBX, FusionPBX and other standards-based SIP platforms.
- Carriers: Twilio, Telnyx, Bandwidth and other carriers.

See the [SIP Integration Overview](sip-integration.md).

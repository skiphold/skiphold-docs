# SkipHold™: The White-Label AI Communications Platform

SkipHold™ is a partner-driven, white-label AI communications platform and framework that service providers deliver end-to-end to their customers under their own brand. AI and human teams work every conversation together: voice, SMS and WhatsApp.

It's built for MSPs, UCaaS providers, ITSPs and telecom partners. You choose native or bring-your-own at every layer: PBX, carrier, CRM and billing. Your stack, our framework (SkipHold Unified). SkipHold deploys, trains and supports the offering, so you don't have to build it.

## White-label AI receptionist and AI voice agents
- Always-on AI agents that answer calls in a natural, conversational voice.
- Three agent types (see [AI voice agent types](https://skiphold.com/ai-voice-agent-types)):
  - **Receptionist:** the company's front door.
  - **Manager:** one per department.
  - **Assistant:** a personal agent for each employee.
- Agent roles: General Receptionist, Appointment Setter, Lead Qualifier, Document Sender, Support Intake, After Hours, General Personal Assistant and General Manager.
- **Prompt Tools™:** a visual prompt builder with templates and role-based context.
- Voices from ElevenLabs and other leading voice providers. Agents speak English, Spanish and Portuguese.
- Agents qualify callers, capture contact details and set appointments via connected calendars, n8n / Zapier MCP automations or your own APIs.
- Test any agent from the portal with a test call or a test chat before it goes live.
- More at [white-label AI receptionist](https://skiphold.com/white-label-ai-receptionist).

## Conversations: omni-channel voice, SMS and WhatsApp
- One inbox where AI and human teams work every conversation together, across voice, SMS and WhatsApp.
- Staff can take over a thread from the AI agent at any time.
- During a call, agents can send the caller an SMS or WhatsApp message.
- WhatsApp message templates, plus A2P 10DLC compliance support for US business messaging.
- See [Conversations](https://skiphold.com/conversations), [AI SMS](https://skiphold.com/integrations/sms) and [AI WhatsApp](https://skiphold.com/integrations/whatsapp).

## Connections: CRM and help desk
- A built-in CRM and help desk for each company: contacts, companies, tasks, deals with multiple pipelines and stages, tickets and internal notes.
- Also includes tags, custom fields, quick replies and message templates.
- Learn more: [Connections](https://skiphold.com/connections).

## Native PBX, web phone and softphones
- [Gemelos PBX](https://skiphold.com/native-pbx): SkipHold's native hosted PBX.
- Branded desktop and mobile softphones, plus a built-in web phone.
- Video between internal extensions; external calls are audio-only.
- AI agents are reachable from users' softphone directory.

## AI warm transfers and call routing
- AI-driven handoffs: Receptionist → department Manager → employee Assistant, or to a live person.
- Blind transfers and **AI warm transfers**: in a warm transfer, the person picking up hears an AI summary of the call first. See [AI warm transfers](https://skiphold.com/warm-transfers).
- Call screening options, such as letting the person accept or decline the call, or announcing it before connecting.
- Fallback rules for transfers that go unanswered.
- A visual call routing map for each company.
- The caller's ID is passed through to agents and transfer destinations.
- Live call monitoring for providers and company admins.
- MSP routing examples: [AI call routing for MSPs](https://skiphold.com/ai-call-routing-for-msps).

## Meeting Join™
- AI agents join Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls by dialing in and entering meeting IDs/PINs via DTMF.
- See [Meeting Join™](https://skiphold.com/ai-meeting-participant).

## White-label Stripe billing
- An optional billing module, so providers can sell to their customers:
  - product catalog and bundles
  - proposals your customers can review and accept
  - invoices and payment collection
- Two billing modes, both fully white-label (your branding on proposals, invoices, statements and the portal):
  - **Platform Billing:** charges are processed on SkipHold's Stripe account, so the card transaction shows "SkipHold, LLC" on the cardholder's statement. SkipHold handles collections and pays you your revenue share.
  - **Direct Billing:** you use your own Stripe account, so your own business name shows on the card statement. You pay SkipHold the platform fee.
- See [provider billing](https://skiphold.com/billing) and [pricing](https://skiphold.com/pricing).

## White-label branding and multi-tenant structure
- Your logo and brand colors, in light and dark mode.
- Your own custom domain for the portal.
- Branded email notifications with your sender name, reply-to address and footer.
- Providers manage many customer companies from one account.

## How SkipHold connects to your phone system

    Carrier (Twilio, Telnyx, Bandwidth, …)  ─┐
    Gemelos PBX                              ├──►  SkipHold SIP Bridge  ──►  SkipHold AI agents
    Your PBX / UCaaS platform              ─┘     (SkipHold Telephony       (Receptionist, Manager,
                                                    servers)                   Assistant)

1. Calls arrive from a carrier number, from Gemelos PBX, or from your existing PBX / UCaaS platform.
2. The SkipHold SIP Bridge connects using SIP extension registration or a SIP trunk. Your users' phones don't change and there's no hardware to install.
3. The bridge hands each call to the right SkipHold AI agent, along with the caller's ID.
4. When an agent transfers a call, it goes back through your PBX, so your existing extensions, queues and users keep working.
5. You manage provisioning and see live connection status in the Admin Portal, powered by the SkipHold Telephony API.

Full details: [SIP Integration Overview](docs/sip-integration.md).
